About NOVA ARCADE

NOVA ARCADE is an independently developed browser arcade with 30 free-to-play challenges, including survival games, puzzles, racing, reflex tests, and arcade classics.

Our goal is straightforward: bring the spirit of the 1980s arcade into your browser with instant-play games, neon visuals, responsive controls, and games that are genuinely fun to replay.

Free games, no downloads

Pick a game, play in your browser, and challenge yourself to beat your best score. Many machines support both desktop keyboard controls and mobile touch controls. You don't need to register an account.

Original gameplay and ongoing improvements

The collection includes independently implemented browser games and original variations on well-known arcade mechanics. We continue refining physics, challenge progression, controls and visuals across the arcade.

PRIVACY & PLAYER DATA

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026

NOVA ARCADE provides free browser-based games. This page explains what information the site uses in its current version and what will change if advertising is introduced.

Information saved on your device

The games may use your browser's local storage to remember high scores, game progress, sound choices, preferences, or recently played games. That information is stored on your device and is not used to create a player account. You can clear it in your browser's site-data settings; doing so may erase your saved scores.

Hosting and technical data

Our hosting provider may process technical information such as IP addresses, request details, and security logs to operate and protect the website. The site's game code does not require you to provide your name or email address.

Advertising and cookies

Advertising has not been activated on this version of NOVA ARCADE. We do not currently load Google AdSense advertising code. If advertisements are introduced, this policy will be updated to explain the advertising partners, data use, cookies or other storage technologies, and applicable privacy choices before ads are enabled. Where required, an appropriate consent-management system will be provided.

External links

The website may link to external sites, such as our public source repository. Those services have their own privacy policies and practices.

Contact

For privacy questions, please use the method on our contact page. Do not include passwords or sensitive personal information in a public issue.
